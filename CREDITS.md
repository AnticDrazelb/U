# Credits

A big thank-you to everyone whose work made Crema possible.

## The port

**ShiroKlein** brought Cemu to the PS4: the platform layer, Vulkan on the PS4 GPU, the DualShock 4
as a Wii U GamePad, the launcher and in-game menu, the settings system, the recompiler and
scheduler work for the PS4's CPU, and all the testing on real hardware.

## Cemu

At the heart of this app is **[Cemu](https://cemu.info)**, the Wii U emulator from the
[Cemu project](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu), licensed MPL-2.0. The PS4 version is Cemu at
commit `cc6fea125a5d4f559c662d13c5f4af0b6dd0876f` plus
[`patches/cemu-ps4.patch`](patches/cemu-ps4.patch).

Cemu was created by **Exzap** and **Petergov**, and has grown through the work of its community
since it was open-sourced in 2022. Below are its contributors as GitHub lists them, most active
first. More appear in the
[complete contributor graph](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu/graphs/contributors) and in
Cemu's commit history.

## Dolphin for PS4

The launcher, the in-game menu, the menu translations, fonts and sounds are adapted from
**[Dolphin for PS4](https://github.com/iHaiDeeZ/DolphinPS4)**, as are the Mesa back end for the PS4's
GPU and much of the PS4 runtime.

## Data and services

- **[GameTDB](https://www.gametdb.com)** and its contributors: the box art the app downloads.
- **[Cemu graphic packs](https://github.com/cemu-project/cemu_graphic_packs)** and their authors:
  downloaded by the app, not shipped with it.
- **[Pretendo Network](https://pretendo.network)**: the online servers.
